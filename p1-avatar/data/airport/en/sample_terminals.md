# Sample International Airport (SAMPLE DATA)

> This file is invented sample data for testing the pipeline. Replace the `data/airport` folders with the trainer's files.

## Terminals

### Terminal 1
Terminal 1 serves domestic flights. Check-in counters are on the departures level (Level 2). Gates A1 to A30 are in Terminal 1.

### Terminal 2
Terminal 2 serves international flights. Gates B1 to B25 and C1 to C40 are in Terminal 2. Gate C22 is in Concourse C, a 10-minute walk from the security checkpoint, after passport control.

## Getting between terminals
A free shuttle bus runs between Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 every 10 minutes, 24 hours a day. The shuttle stop is outside Door 4 on the arrivals level of each terminal.

## Lounges
The Skyline Lounge is in Terminal 2, Concourse C, opposite Gate C10. It is open from 05:00 to 23:00. Entry is free for business-class passengers and costs 35 US dollars for others.

## Baggage
Lost baggage can be reported at the Baggage Services desk in the arrivals hall of each terminal, next to belt 6. The desk is open 24 hours.

## Wi-Fi
Free Wi-Fi is available in all terminals. Connect to the network "SIA-Free-WiFi" and accept the terms on the welcome page. No password is needed.

## Prayer rooms
Prayer rooms are in Terminal 1 near Gate A12 and in Terminal 2 near Gate B5.
